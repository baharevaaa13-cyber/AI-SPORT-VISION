EVERY MOVEMENT MATTERS

A coach sees the athlete.
AI helps the coach see more.

In a real training session, one coach may supervise several athletes simultaneously. Important technical details can remain unnoticed, especially underwater or during fast phases of movement.

AI SPORT VISION is being developed as a decision-support system: video capture, athlete tracking, movement analysis and clear feedback for the coach.
